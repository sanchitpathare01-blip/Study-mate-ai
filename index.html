FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Is StudyMate AI free to use?

Yes! You can immediately use StudyMate AI with full access to summaries, quizzes, flashcards, and streak tracking. Demo mode is enabled out of the box with zero setup required.

How accurate are the AI summaries and quizzes?

StudyMate AI uses the advanced Google Gemini AI engine (`gemini-2.5-flash`), strictly analyzing the concepts present in your provided text to generate questions, answers, and summaries.

How does the 30-day streak calendar work?

Your streak advances only when you perform meaningful study activity: generating materials, taking a quiz, or grading flashcards. Logging in alone will never falsely credit a day.

Can I review previous study sessions later?

Absolutely. Every session is saved in your Study History library. You can reopen any past session instantly without calling the AI API again.
